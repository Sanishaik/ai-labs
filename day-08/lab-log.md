## Task 4 – Capstone 2: Invoice Validation Using MCP

In Task 4, I developed an invoice validation project using Python and MCP. I worked with purchase order data and created five sample invoice JSON files. I implemented validation to check invoice details against the corresponding purchase orders, including purchase order numbers, vendor IDs, and invoice amounts.

I created an MCP server with tools to retrieve purchase order details, list late purchase orders, check invoice and purchase order information, and validate invoices. I executed the validation tests and received the expected results. Out of five invoices, three were identified as CLEAN and two were identified as EXCEPTION because of vendor and amount mismatches.

I also generated a clean invoice summary, an exceptions report, and an audit log. The audit log records the invoice number, validation status, and purchase order number. This task helped me understand how AI tools and Python can support invoice checking and identify mismatches before further business processing.
