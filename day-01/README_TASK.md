# Day 1 – AI Experiments

## Objective
To understand how AI tools respond to prompts and how to evaluate the accuracy and reliability of their answers.

## Tasks Completed
- Task 1: Prepared SAP-related prompts.
- Task 2: Compared answers from ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini.
- Task 3: Conducted three AI experiments.
- Task 4: Created AI trust and verification rules.
- Task 5: Recorded activities and learning outcomes in the lab log.

## Key Learnings
I learned to compare AI-generated answers, identify possible inaccuracies, and verify important information using reliable sources.
