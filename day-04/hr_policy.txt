HR LEAVE POLICY

4.1 Casual Leave:
Employees are entitled to 12 days of casual leave per year.

4.2 Paternity Leave:
Employees are entitled to 15 working days of paternity leave.

4.3 Sick Leave:
Employees are entitled to 10 days of sick leave per year.
A doctor's note is required after 2 days of sick leave.

4.4 Annual Leave:
Employees are entitled to 20 days of annual leave per year.

4.5 Work From Home:
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval.

4.6 Public Holidays:
Employees receive 10 public holidays per year.

4.7 Leave Approval:
Employees must submit leave requests to their reporting manager.

4.8 Leave Carry Forward:
Employees may carry forward up to 5 unused annual leave days to the next year.

4.9 Emergency Leave:
Employees must inform their reporting manager as soon as possible when emergency leave is required.

4.10 Probation Period:
The probation period for new employees is 6 months.
